CORRECTIONS AND INTERPRETATIONS

Q15: Adopted practice interpretation: count successful loop-condition comparisons/iterations. These are floor(log2(n))+1. Counting the final false condition adds one, giving floor(log2(n))+2, which is absent from the printed options; supplied A fails at powers of two. Original examination scan p4 confirms the same printed defect.

Q24: The ten even numbers are exchangeable; each has probability 1/10 of being first among them.

Q43: A full n-ary tree has nI=I+L-1 edges. With I=10 and L=41, n=50/10=5.

Q48: Every ordinary nonempty disjunctive clause is satisfied by a uniformly random assignment with probability at least 1/2. Expectation guarantees an assignment satisfying at least half the clauses. C contradicts this guarantee.

Q51: Even composite n stops after its first divisor check, while prime n performs Theta(sqrt(n)) checks. Thus a uniform upper bound is O(sqrt(n)) and lower bound Omega(1). Treat the code as the intended C-like primality pseudocode despite its redundant printed declaration.

Q15 is intrinsically defective as printed, including in the original scan.
For n>0, the body executes floor(log2(n))+1 times. Testing the condition once
more to leave the loop gives floor(log2(n))+2 comparisons, absent from all
options. The quiz visibly asks for successful loop-condition comparisons
(iterations), for which D is exact. The crop is unchanged. This qualification
is a practice interpretation, not a claim about an official awarded answer.

Q78/Q79 source restoration: supplied paper page 18 ends the grammar with
S -> bAA. The original examination page 20 correctly has A -> bAA.
The quiz uses an unchanged crop of the entire original common statement and
grammar. Source page size is 557.640015 x 825.47998 PDF points; crop rectangle
[45,29,498,157] PDF points, rendered at 240 DPI. Question/options remain from
the supplied paper page 18. Under the correct grammar only aabbab (C) among
Q78 choices is generated, with exactly two derivation trees (Q79 B). Both answers
also happen to be unchanged under the literal reproduction typo.

Other historical/wording conventions retained:
  Q44: A is the usual worst-case recursion-depth answer. Actual calls can be
  zero when m divides n; the question omits the phrase worst case.
  Q54: B assumes dependency-preserving code generation for the given basic block,
  rather than arbitrary algebraic reassociation of its mathematical expression.
  Q58: D is the supplied and intended most specific option: mutual exclusion is
  ensured but simultaneous intent can deadlock. Bounded waiting consequently
  also fails; the single-choice wording is imperfect.
  Q67: C uses the historical all-zero/all-one subnet exclusion (62 subnets,
  1022 hosts). Modern 64 subnets / 1022 hosts is absent from the options.
  Q69: A uses the conventional Go-Back-N sliding-window interpretation. The
  question leaves the protocol variant implicit.
  Q70: B classifies BGP by its network-routing function. BGP uses TCP; the
  other three layer matches make B the intended printed choice.

DERIVATIONS FOR COMPLETED BLANKS

Q4 (B): K3,3 is non-planar with 9 edges and 6 vertices. Every non-planar graph has a subdivision of K5 or K3,3, requiring at least 9 edges; K5 requires 10.

Q8 (C): Use one 3-to-8 decoder on the high 3 bits to enable eight decoders of the low 3 bits: 1+8=9.

Q9 (B): The listed minterms are exactly x XOR z. Both w and y are independent variables.

Q10 (D): 128 lines / 4 ways = 32 sets, so set/LINE bits=5. A line of 64 words needs 6 WORD bits. TAG=20−5−6=9.

Q11 (A): Capacity = 16×128×256×512 = 2^28 bytes = 256 MiB. There are 2^(4+7+8)=2^19 sectors, requiring 19 sector-address bits.

Q16 (A): Gang scheduling schedules related threads together (3); rate-monotonic scheduling is real-time scheduling (2); fair-share scheduling supplies scheduling shares/guarantees (1).

Q17 (D): The kernel can block one kernel-level thread while scheduling another; blocking one does not block all related kernel threads.

Q19 (A): Manchester uses two signal elements per data bit, so bit rate is half the conventional signal-element/baud rate.

Q20 (C): DNS uses UDP for ordinary queries, while the listed HTTP, Telnet and SMTP protocols use TCP in the historical context of the paper. DNS can also use TCP.

Q21 (A): The two isomorphism classes are cyclic C4 and the Klein four-group C2×C2.

Q23 (C): The complement of C25 is connected and every vertex has degree 24−2=22, so it has an Eulerian circuit. An arbitrary even-regular graph need not be connected; K90 has odd degree 89.

Q25 (C): For Av=λv, vectors (v,v) and (v,−v) give eigenvalues λ+1 and λ−1 of the block matrix. Its spectrum is {−6,−4,−3,−1,0,2,3,5}; only option 2 occurs.

Q27 (A): Both vectors lie in the plane x1+x2+x3=0 and are independent. Every vector in that plane equals (−x2)(1,−1,0)+(−x3)(1,0,−1).

Q32 (D): Truth-table enumeration gives P=Q=R={0,4,5,7,8,9,13,15}, while S={0,2,3,6,7,8,9}. Thus S only is not equivalent.

Q34 (C): Choose n−1 variables as select lines of a 2^(n−1):1 mux. Each residual one-variable function is 0,1,x or NOT x, with one shared inverter enough. Fewer select lines leave arbitrary two-variable functions at data inputs, which cannot all be supplied with one inverter.

Q35 (B): Expanding C1 through C4 in parallel gives respectively 1,2,3,4 nontrivial AND product terms and one OR gate per carry. Hence 10 AND and 4 OR gates; the sum XOR gates are outside the requested AND/OR count.

Q36 (C): The diagram connects A3 and A1 to an AND feeding asynchronous Clear. The first count asserting both is binary 0101=5, which immediately clears with negligible delay. Stable observed sequence is 0,1,2,3,4.

Q37 (B): ADD IF1 ID2 EX3 WB4; MUL IF2 ID3 EX4–6 WB7; SUB IF3 ID4–6 (held) EX7 WB8. Forwarding removes additional data stalls; completion is cycle 8.

Q56 (B): P is Belady's anomaly for FIFO. Q can be true, but lack of locality is not the reason FIFO can fault more when its frame allocation increases.

Q57 (C): The actual image table gives allocated vectors P0=(1,2,1), P1=(2,0,1), P2=(2,2,1), hence available=(0,1,2). Only P1 can finish first; available becomes (2,1,3), allowing P0; P2 finishes last. Text extraction misaligns P0's row.

Q59 (B): The Cartesian product lists every female student/course pair. Removing enroll leaves missing female enrolments. Projection returns courses missing at least one female student; since at least one female is enrolled in each, their female enrolments form a proper subset.

Q60 (C): For every employee x, if e directly supervises x then x is male. Thus e has no immediate female subordinate, including employees with no subordinates.

Q61 (A): Q1 excludes e precisely if some department-5 employee has salary greater than or equal to e's salary, so it selects salaries above the department maximum. Q2 uses ANY, requiring only one smaller salary. Conventional non-null salary model is implicit in the exam.

Q62 (D): BCNF disallows a nontrivial dependency with a non-superkey determinant, preventing the nonkey-intermediary transitive dependency intended here. 3NF permits it for a prime right-hand attribute. Two-attribute relations are BCNF under the usual exam assumptions, and single-attribute candidate keys cannot have partial dependencies.

Q63 (A): A leaf reserves a 6-byte next-leaf block pointer. Each value/record-pointer pair takes 9+7=16 bytes. floor((1024-6)/16)=63.

Q64 (C): S1 has T1->T2 through Y and T2->T1 through X, a cycle. S2 has only T2->T1, hence is conflict serializable in order T2,T1.

Q65 (A): There are n choices of the sole transmitter, each with probability p(1-p)^(n-1), assuming independent attempts.

Q66 (C): One bit lasts 1/10^7 second=0.1 microsecond. At 200 metres/microsecond this equals 20 metres.

Q67 (C): The historical convention intended by these options excludes all-zero and all-one subnet identifiers: 2^6-2=62. Class B leaves 16-6=10 host bits, hence 2^10-2=1022 hosts.

Q68 (B): Modulo-2 division of 11001001000 by generator 1001 yields remainder 011, so append 011 to obtain 11001001011.

Q69 (A): With propagation Lt and frame transmission K/R, continuous operation needs a sender window at least 1+2LtR/K. The conventional Go-Back-N sequence space is at least one greater than its window, giving ceil(log2((2LtR+2K)/K)).

Q70 (B): SMTP is application layer, TCP transport, and PPP data link. Only option B has those three matches; it places BGP at the network layer by routing function.

Q80 (C): Start 0x1100=4352 is 64-byte aligned, block68. The 2500-byte array spans 40 blocks,68..107. The first scan has40 misses. Eight direct-mapped line collisions overwrite blocks68..75 with100..107. Scan2 misses on both these groups,16 misses. Total56.

Q81 (A): Blocks68..75 and100..107 both map modulo32 to lines4..11, the lines replaced in scan2. Other array lines retain their matching blocks.

Q82 (A): Optimal faults on references1,2,3,7,4,5,6 only. At7 evict2 (never used again), then evict each one-use replacement while retaining1 and3. Final3 and1 hit:7 faults.

Q83 (C): LRU faults on every access except the second reference to1:9 faults. Optimal has7, so LRU has2 more.
