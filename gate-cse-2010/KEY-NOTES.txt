Corrections to nonblank screenshot entries
Q7 D -> B: chips operate in parallel. Refreshing 1K rows takes
1024 x 100 ns = 102400 ns; multiplying by the number of chips/cells is wrong.
Q18 C -> B: five maximum internal keys imply six maximum children. A nonroot
internal node needs at least ceil(6/2)=3 children, hence two keys. The minimum
over any nonroot node is two; the leaf minimum does not make C correct.
Q30 D -> B: forall x exists y exists t not F(x,y,t) is equivalent to
not exists x forall y forall t F(x,y,t): no one can fool everyone all the time.
Q34 A -> B: skip a0 to get Y, or include a0 and halve all tail weights to get
a0 + Y/2. The answer is max(Y, a0 + Y/2). The original defines Y explicitly.
Q49 A -> C: the L2 block has 16 words but the memory bus carries four words.
Four transfers cost 4 x (200+20) ns, then L2-to-L1 costs 20+2 ns: total 902 ns.
Q54 D -> C: two edges are unused by shortest paths: R1-R2 has weight 6 versus
the alternative R1-R3-R2 of weight 5; R4-R6 has weight 8 versus R4-R5-R6 of 5.
Q55 A -> B: after changing those two weights to 2, R5-R6 (weight 4) is unused
because R5-R4-R6 costs 3. Exactly one edge is unused.

Missing/contradictory screenshot entries
Q37 blank -> B: a, b and c are live just before d=a+b, requiring three
registers. Reuse registers when operands die; three registers suffice.
Q53 blank -> C: exhaustive enumeration of all 6! insertion orders yields
30 orders with the printed final linear-probing table. Q52 is C.
Q60 is printed twice, with A and C. Use A only: an unemployed worker and
fallow land are both available productive resources not currently in use.
Q65 retains B: the original has nine digits 2,2,3,3,3,4,4,4,4. There are
25 allowed numbers beginning with 3 and 26 beginning with 4, totaling 51.
The supplied reproduction omits one 3, which would yield 44, absent from
the printed options. Use the original question crop, not the defective list.
