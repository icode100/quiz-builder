65 questions: 38 MCQ and 27 NAT; 100 marks.
Original order: GA 1-10, then CS 1-55. Printed headers identify GA Set-6
and CS Set B. No session number or date is inferred.

Start ../start-quiz.command on macOS or ../start-quiz.cmd on Windows, then open:
http://127.0.0.1:8765/gate-cse-2016-set-2/
Node.js is required; no npm packages, build, account or internet is needed.
Use the same browser/profile and 127.0.0.1:8765 origin for draft recovery.

The current template provides automatic draft recovery (including your last
question, review flags, syllabus exclusions and timer), the practice carousel,
question map, light/dark theme, shared calculator and per-question rough work.
On narrow screens, scroll question images sideways to read at normal zoom.
Timer presets are 180, 150, 120, 90, 60 and 30 minutes. At zero you may keep
answering. Submit resets the timer before checking answers.

Submit grades the paper and saves only the latest attempt.json. The draft
clears only after a successful save. Invalid numerical input or a server
failure preserves it. Review last attempt recomputes the saved report without
replacing the current draft; Back to quiz restores the previous view.
Attempt again starts fresh practice and preserves the saved submission.
Save report and Print report are available. Browser-data removal may erase
unfinished answers and rough work. Rough-work notes are separate from quiz
answers; Submit, Clear answers and Attempt again do not remove them.

Wrong MCQ answers deduct exactly one-third of the question marks. NAT has no
negative marking. Ordinary unanswered questions score zero. Marking follows
the IISc-authored GATE 2016 Information Brochure, sections 5.4 and 5.4.6,
read at this third-party mirror of the primary brochure:
https://myexam.allen.in/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/Brochure_GATE-2016-official.pdf
CS-6 accepts 0.124-0.126; CS-33 3.9-4.1; CS-39 2.2-2.4; CS-47 8.2-8.3,
all inclusive. Other numerical answers are exact, including CS-7 = -1.
No marks-to-all awards, alternate MCQ answers or linked dependencies.
Without exclusions: blank 0/100, perfect 100/100, all wrong -19/100.

Out of syllabus manually excludes a question from earned/eligible marks;
its answer and review flag remain. Percentage is earned/eligible marks x 100,
shown before marks. All excluded means percentage unavailable and 0/0 marks.
Clear response keeps its flag and exclusion; Clear answers and fresh Attempt
again clear them. No notebook state enters drafts or saved submissions.
